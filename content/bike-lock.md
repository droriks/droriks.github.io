# Biometric Bike Lock
Dates: Built
Role: [solo / team of N, my part was...]
Tools & Skills: Arduino Uno, SolidWorks, Fingerprint sensor, Bluetooth, 3D printing, Soldering

## Summary
A bike lock that only unlocks for the right fingerprint and the right key fob; a DC motor and worm gear drive a locking pin through the spokes.

## Details
Worked reliably; the Bluetooth module was hard to connect to the laptop reliably (a better module would help). As a product: sheet metal or aluminum housing, waterproofing for the electronics, and drop the fingerprint sensor (cool, but hurts ease of use).
