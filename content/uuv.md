# Fish-Inspired UUV
Dates: Summer 2026
Role: Undergraduate Researcher, UMD Hydrodynamics Lab, advised by Professor Cecilia Huertas-Cerdeira (ASPIRE Scholarship)
Tools & Skills: SolidWorks, CNC milling, Waterjet, KiCad, Raspberry Pi, ROS 2, Python

## Summary
An untethered, fish-inspired UUV whose tail fin is driven by a 4-DOF parallel manipulator (3RRS platform + roll servo), aiming for both efficient long-distance swimming and tight maneuvering. Fin moves within a 50° cone with 180° roll; tested underwater with sine-wave gaits. Presented at SURC.
