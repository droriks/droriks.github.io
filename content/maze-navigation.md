# Autonomous Maze Navigation
Dates: Built
Role: Team of 3 ([my part was...])
Tools & Skills: ROS 2, Python, TurtleBot 4, LiDAR, SLAM, AMCL, Nav2, RViz, ros2 bag

## Summary
A TurtleBot 4 escapes a maze in two timed races: SLAM + AMCL + Nav2 for the mapped race, and a ROS 2 LiDAR right-hand wall follower with a three-state machine for the unmapped race.
